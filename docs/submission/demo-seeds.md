## Selection procedure

1. Resolve all five concepts against actual Qloo data and document the chosen IDs/types/ambiguities.
2. Require distinct identities and at least three supported cultural domains; four are preferred.
3. Observe destination/place/brand or verified supported equivalents without inventing relationships.
4. Choose one profile for clarity, provenance and reproducibility, not for a desired ALLOW.
5. Report natural score/decision distributions honestly. A profile need not produce every policy class.

These profiles are proposed alternatives to the earlier illustrative set, not a claim that they differ from every example Qloo has ever published.
