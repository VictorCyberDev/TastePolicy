# Investor FAQ and founder narrative

## Who is building TastePolicy?

Victor is building TastePolicy as an independent founder project. The product's existing accounts, repository and evidence are attributable in the company ledger. No corporation, employee count, prior exit, employer endorsement or credential is invented. The founder story is a product insight: an assistant can remember what someone likes and still cross the boundary on what it may choose. The build explores how to make that boundary explicit, inspectable and portable across agents.

## Is this a Qloo wrapper?

Qloo owns the cultural intelligence. TastePolicy adds explicit state, scoped grants, contextual rules, action ceilings, four decision outcomes, leases and proofs. The business must prove that this separate authority layer earns a buyer budget. No exclusive access to Qloo or blanket right to commercialize its output is claimed.

## Is the product safe?

It has tested controls and material limitations. Historic proof validity, current authority and execution permission are separate. The descriptive pilot has high missed ASK and does not establish safety. No certification or formal proof covers the whole service. See the threat model, current CI and pilot report.

## Does it work in ChatGPT and Claude?

Remote MCP and an MCP Apps resource are implemented. Real assistant commissioning is unproved until signed-in conversations invoke the tools and evidence is captured. Compatibility, installation, scan success, invocation, submission, review, approval and publication are separate states.

## What traction exists?

Verified engineering acceptance and a limited descriptive pilot. No verified paying customers, revenue or contracted pipeline are included. The 100-paying-customer goal is a target.

## Can an agent use ALLOW to buy or book?

No. ALLOW covers the specified subjective action within a ceiling. Financial, booking, messaging, account, disclosure and legal authority require their own execution controls. Verification does not confer that authority.

## Why will buyers pay rather than use a prompt?

The hypothesis is that auditable boundaries, revocation, commitments and reusable user control reduce integration failures. That hypothesis requires paid pilots and held-out baseline comparisons. If a prompt with the same evidence achieves equivalent outcomes at lower cost, the proposed differentiation is insufficient.

## What is the fundraising ask?

The accompanying memo models USD500,000 over 18 months as a draft planning scenario. Terms, instrument, ownership, jurisdiction, cap table and final ask need founder/legal decisions. No financing commitment or offer exists from this package.

## What must be proven next?

Written provider rights and costs, usable multi-tenant onboarding, sandbox payment-to-entitlement acceptance, real assistants, submitted distribution, restoration/monitoring operations, improved held-out authority evaluation and actual retained paid use.
