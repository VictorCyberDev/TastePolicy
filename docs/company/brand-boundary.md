# Boundary visual system

Version 1, 2026-10-28. Authored vector assets; no third-party logo pack or generated stock imagery. Mark and wordmark are project identifiers, not a trademark registration.

## Form and meaning

Two brackets protect a deliberate central gap. Taste evidence lives on one side; explicit permission lives on the other. The gap is the decision boundary. The mark never implies that Qloo, OpenAI or Anthropic endorses TastePolicy.

Assets: `public/brand/mark.svg`, `mark.png` (512 px), `wordmark.svg`, `social-card.svg`, `social-card.png` (1200×630) and `src/app/icon.svg`. PNGs are deterministic raster exports of the authored SVGs. OpenGraph/Twitter metadata references the production asset paths.

Color is accompanied by text/icon state, never the only signal. Evidence bars illustrate cultural fit; they do not depict permission or confidence in authorization. Receipt, lease and proof state remain separately labeled. Example cards explicitly say no live decision, lease or proof was issued.

Inter supports product/body text; Instrument Serif supports expressive headings. Font licenses are bundled under `public/fonts/OFL-*`. Type scale: 10/12/14/16 body/interface; 29/38/48/64/88 headings, responsive. Spacing uses 4/8/12/16/20/24/32/40/56/72/88. Component radii are restrained (3–8 px), with thin visible boundary rules.

## Motion and access

Only short 180 ms positional hover affordances. No ambient animation, autoplay or decision theater. Reduced-motion removes transforms and transitions. Keyboard focus is visible; marketing navigation begins with a skip link. Light/dark follows the OS without saving tracking preferences. Core text and buttons remain available without decorative SVGs.

## Surfaces

CompanyShell owns marketing navigation/footer. CompanyDocument owns legal/support/docs layout. AuthorityPreview is an explicitly illustrative four-outcome learning component. Real account policy, lease, receipt and proof interfaces remain connected to their existing APIs. An example UI is not a real MCP-hosted interactive app; that commissioning remains M16/M17.
