# TastePolicy

**Subjective Authority Infrastructure for AI Agents**

TastePolicy places a user-controlled boundary between what an agent thinks someone might like and what it may choose for that person. Qloo supplies cultural evidence. TastePolicy combines that evidence with explicit rules, contextual limits and a permitted action ceiling, producing ALLOW, ASK, REJECT or UNAVAILABLE with an inspectable Cultural Receipt. Bounded leases and portable proofs let other agents check the captured authority and its current status. Verification remains separate from authorization, and preference authority never grants payment, booking, messaging or legal authority.

The initial buyer hypothesis is a small team building a travel or concierge agent that already proposes places. The first integration covers a reviewed candidate and draft recommendation before a separate execution/approval system. Adjacent shopping, gifting and entertainment opportunities remain hypotheses.

Those results identify work to do; they establish neither population safety nor a win over competing approaches.
